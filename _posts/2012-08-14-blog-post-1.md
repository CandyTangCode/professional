🌟 Meet Shujun Tang: Turning Data into Impact, One Decision at a Time

Hello everyone — I’m Shujun (Candy) Tang, and I’m thrilled to share a bit about my journey with you.

From the start, my path has been guided by curiosity, purpose, and a deep belief in the power of data to drive meaningful change. Whether optimizing operations at Amazon, shaping product strategy for a SaaS startup, or advancing neuroscience research, I’ve always asked one question:
“How can we use data to make smarter, more human-centered decisions?”

🚀 My Journey So Far

I began my career as a biomedical engineer, fascinated by how data could reveal hidden insights in complex systems. Over the years, I’ve transitioned across industries — from biotech and pharmaceuticals to tech startups and logistics — driven by a desire to bridge technical analysis with strategic leadership.

At Amazon Logistics, I manage daily operations for 100+ associates, leading data-driven labor planning that cut inefficiencies by 35% and improved safety inspection speed by 25%.

At Stack Moxie, a B2B SaaS startup, I partnered with customers, analyzed usage data, and prototyped new features using Figma, aligning cross-functional teams through Amazon-style PRFAQs and white papers.

At Nitecapp, I crafted a pricing strategy projected to add $52M in revenue and built valuation models that uncovered $8M in hidden value.

Earlier, in my research roles at Allen Institute and Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, I designed data-driven experiments that improved sensitivity by 7x, reduced turnaround times from 3 days to 1, and helped secure over $160M in funding.

Each chapter taught me that data is only powerful when it leads to action — and that true leadership means translating complex insights into strategies people believe in.

💡 What I Believe In

I believe great product and program managers are storytellers of data — we connect numbers to narratives, analysis to empathy, and performance metrics to human impact.

To me, success isn’t just about efficiency or growth. It’s about designing processes that empower people, building products that solve real problems, and leading teams with clarity, compassion, and curiosity.

📚 Lifelong Learning

I’m currently pursuing my MBA at the University of Washington’s Foster School of Business, deepening my expertise in finance, strategy, and machine learning. As a two-time Meritorious Winner of the International Mathematical Contest in Modeling, I’ve always enjoyed tackling complex problems — blending quantitative rigor with creative problem-solving.

I’m also proud to serve as Treasurer and Board Member at the Washington Council for Economic and Financial Education, where we equip K–12 educators to bring financial literacy into classrooms across the state.

🌍 My Vision

My goal is to continue building a career at the intersection of data, strategy, and leadership — leading teams that use technology and analytics to make businesses smarter and societies stronger.

Long term, I aspire to launch a family foundation dedicated to empowering young women entering college — combining my love for education, finance, and social impact.

✨ Outside of Work

When I’m not diving into dashboards or brainstorming product roadmaps, you’ll often find me playing the Chinese flute, a passion I’ve had since childhood. It’s my way of staying grounded — a quiet reminder that beauty and balance matter as much as precision and performance.

Thank you for reading my story. I’m always excited to connect with people who share a passion for data, innovation, and purpose-driven leadership.
Let’s build something meaningful — together.
