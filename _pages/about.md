Candy (Shujun) Tang is a data-driven product and program management leader with 8+ years of cross-functional experience spanning operations, SaaS product strategy, finance, and biomedical innovation. She integrates advanced analytics, technical expertise, and strategic execution to drive measurable impact across diverse industries.

At Amazon Logistics, Candy leads data-informed labor planning and performance management for 100+ associates, leveraging SQL analytics, statistical modeling, and process optimization to achieve a 35% reduction in labor inefficiencies and 25% faster vehicle safety inspections. Her insights guide real-time staffing, operational improvements, and cost-effective decision-making.

As a Product Management Consultant at Stack Moxie, a B2B SaaS startup, she conducted quantitative customer analysis, feature validation, and gap assessments, using data to influence roadmap priorities. She prototyped new features in Figma, authored Amazon-style PRFAQs and white papers, and aligned cross-functional teams around a unified product vision.

In her finance internship at Nitecapp, Candy applied pricing analytics, willingness-to-pay assessments, and benchmarking to design a revenue strategy projected to generate $52M in additional revenue, and led a valuation modeling project that surfaced $8M in hidden value creation.

Her earlier scientific roles at Allen Institute, Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, and Abbott Laboratories highlight her strength in data modeling, experimental design, and performance analytics — improving detection sensitivity by 7x, shortening turnaround time by 3x, and supporting $160M+ in secured funding.

Recognized as a two-time Meritorious Winner in the International Mathematical Contest in Modeling (a global data and analytics competition equivalent to a national-level Data Prize), Candy demonstrates deep analytical rigor and problem-solving excellence.

A Green Card holder through the National Interest Waiver, she is skilled in SQL, machine learning, and data visualization, with a passion for transforming data into actionable strategy. Beyond work, Candy serves as Treasurer and Board Member for the Washington Council for Economic and Financial Education, advancing statewide financial literacy through data-backed program design and fundraising.
